## Found while implementing, with no owner

Two differences from MuJoCo 3.5.0 that the review of Rigid-physics chunk 2b (P09–P14) found and no entry of 20-rigid-physics owns. Neither is isolated. Chunk 3 (P24–P53) assigns each an owner entry before its first commit.

- **Inverse finite differences under a constraint** (ledger-L66). The first model of `hybrid_takes_finite_differences_under_an_active_constraint` (`sim/L0/tests/integration/derivatives.rs`): a damped hinge at `qpos` 0.12, past its range ±0.1, one constraint row. Forward differences with eps 1e-6, measured at `main` `1bd103bc`: sim-core's `mjd_inverse_fd` gives DfDq 225.03, DfDv 9.0057, DfDa 0.090007; MuJoCo's `mjd_inverseFD` gives 4500.66, 180.115, 1.80015, each 20 times ours to eight significant digits. The forward states agree to 1e-13 (`qacc` −294.284, `qfrc_constraint` −28.9226 in both). The two take the constraint force differently: sim-core subtracts the force the forward solve left (`sim/L0/core/src/inverse.rs:52`), MuJoCo computes it from `qacc` (`mj_invConstraint`, `engine_inverse.c:223`); whether that accounts for the difference is not measured. Without a constraint they agree (`inverse_finite_differences_match_mujoco_3_5_0_without_constraints`). No entry owns inverse dynamics.
- **A box resting on a plane** (ledger-L68). Census doc `2529ce5bef3785f7`, the second model of the same test, verdict `e1:state@30`: under the first initial velocity, the state first differs at the step-30 checkpoint. Not isolated.

Rigid-physics chunk 3a (the hybrid-versus-finite-differences sweep and its fixes) found two differences that have owners, whose entries say what it measured: P30 (the implicit integrators and the mass matrix's sparsity) and P28 (the kinematics' quaternion arithmetic). These have no owner entry yet:

- **Negative tendon stiffness and damping** (ledger-L97; measured on the oracle). MuJoCo 3.5.0 loads them (`tendon_damping` −0.5 on the oracle) and applies them with no sign test (`engine_passive.c:454-473`). sim-mjcf refuses them (`validate_tendon_params`, `sim/L0/mjcf/src/validation.rs`), and sim-core skips a tendon's nonpositive stiffness and damping in its passive pass and its nonpositive damping in `mjd_passive_vel`, which no MJCF reaches while the loader refuses. A negative tendon frictionloss MuJoCo also loads, but ignores: it makes a frictionloss row only for a positive one (`engine_core_constraint.c:737`), so that refusal stands, as 11-settled A3-O4 refuses negative muscle parameters. A negative joint damping matches MuJoCo (beside a positive one under Euler, `euler_damps_every_awake_dof_as_mujoco_3_5_0`; a single damped dof under implicitfast and implicit, 20 steps, to 5e-16). Loading negative stiffness and damping (parity) or keeping the refusal as a stated stricter row is open.
- **An applied wrench in the hybrid's position columns** (ledger-L99; measured in sim-core by the chunk's review). `xfrc_applied` adds `Jᵀw`, whose dependence on q the hybrid's analytic position columns leave out: on a hinge with a wrench, A was off pure finite differences under Euler, implicitfast and implicitspringdamper (full Implicit takes its position columns by finite differences). It is a `Data` input, so `forces_outside_the_analytic_columns`, a `Model` predicate, cannot route it. sim-coupling relies on the hybrid leaving it out: `analytic_state_jacobian` adds `∂(Jᵀw)/∂q` itself (`sim/L1/coupling/src/articulated.rs`), so routing such a state to finite differences would count it twice there.
- **sim-gpu and the other model options** (ledger-L100; measured by the chunk's review). sim-gpu follows the damper and eulerdamp flags (chunk 3a) and, on T33's free body, none of the other options the CPU step reads that were tried: with `DISABLE_GRAVITY` it still applies gravity, and it applies no joint stiffness. Implicitfast matched there; full Implicit and RK4 were not run. `GpuPhysicsPipeline::new` refuses only more than 60 dofs and joints other than free.
- **A flex edge shorter than 1e-10** (ledger-L101; by reading, not run). sim-core skips such an edge: no force, and a zero Jacobian row (chunk 3a). MuJoCo normalizes any edge longer than 1e-15 and, below that, takes the x axis as its direction (`mju_normalize3`, `engine_util_blas.c:120-135`).
- **The damper and eulerdamp flags in sim-coupling** (ledger-L102; by reading, not run). sim-coupling builds `M + Δt·D` from `implicit_damping` whatever the flags (`sim/L1/coupling/src/vjp.rs` `implicit_mass_inverse`, and twice in `articulated.rs`), where sim-core's step and transition derivative solve with `M` where `eulerdamp_applies` is false (chunk 3a). No in-tree model sets either flag there.
