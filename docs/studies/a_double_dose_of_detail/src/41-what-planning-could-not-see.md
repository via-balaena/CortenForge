- **Inverse finite differences under a constraint** (ledger-L66). The first model of `hybrid_takes_finite_differences_under_an_active_constraint` (`sim/L0/tests/integration/derivatives.rs`): a damped hinge at `qpos` 0.12, past its range ±0.1, one constraint row. The two take the constraint force differently: sim-core subtracts the force the forward solve left (`sim/L0/core/src/inverse.rs:52`), MuJoCo computes it from `qacc` (`mj_invConstraint`, `engine_inverse.c:223`); whether that accounts for the difference is not measured. Without a constraint they agree (`inverse_finite_differences_match_mujoco_3_5_0_without_constraints`). No entry owns inverse dynamics.
