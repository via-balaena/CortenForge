## Found while implementing, with no owner

Two differences from MuJoCo 3.5.0 that the review of Rigid-physics chunk 2b (P09–P14) found and no entry of 20-rigid-physics owns. Neither is isolated. Chunk 3 (P24–P53) assigns each an owner entry before its first commit.

- **Inverse finite differences under a constraint** (ledger-L66). The first model of `hybrid_takes_finite_differences_under_an_active_constraint` (`sim/L0/tests/integration/derivatives.rs`): a damped hinge at `qpos` 0.12, past its range ±0.1, one constraint row. Forward differences with eps 1e-6, measured at `main` `1bd103bc`: sim-core's `mjd_inverse_fd` gives DfDq 225.03, DfDv 9.0057, DfDa 0.090007; MuJoCo's `mjd_inverseFD` gives 4500.66, 180.115, 1.80015, each 20 times ours to eight significant digits. The forward states agree to 1e-13 (`qacc` −294.284, `qfrc_constraint` −28.9226 in both). The two take the constraint force differently: sim-core subtracts the force the forward solve left (`sim/L0/core/src/inverse.rs:52`), MuJoCo computes it from `qacc` (`mj_invConstraint`, `engine_inverse.c:223`); whether that accounts for the difference is not measured. Without a constraint they agree (`inverse_finite_differences_match_mujoco_3_5_0_without_constraints`). No entry owns inverse dynamics.
- **A box resting on a plane** (ledger-L68). Census doc `2529ce5bef3785f7`, the second model of the same test, verdict `e1:state@30`: under the first initial velocity, the state first differs at the step-30 checkpoint. Not isolated.

Found in Rigid-physics chunk 3a (the hybrid-versus-finite-differences sweep and its fixes), with no owner entry yet.

- **implicitfast and the mass matrix's sparsity** (ledger-L92). MuJoCo 3.5.0's implicitfast step gathers `qDeriv` onto the mass matrix's sparsity pattern (`engine_forward.c:1180-1189`), and that pattern leaves out structural zeros: for one box on a ball or free joint it has no entry between the joint's dofs. So MuJoCo drops the cross terms a velocity actuator's moment over those dofs puts in `qDeriv`; sim-core keeps them. Measured: rebuilding MuJoCo's step with `qDeriv` restricted to that pattern gives its `qvel` exactly; with the whole `qDeriv`, 1e-2 off. Whether to match MuJoCo here (porting its sparsity) is open; `joint_transmission.rs` pins the 9 cases as still differing. Whether the same drop reaches other couplings (tendon damping across branches) is not measured.
- **The kinematics' quaternion arithmetic** (ledger-L91). MuJoCo's `mj_kinematics` normalizes, turns and multiplies quaternions with `mju_normalize4`, `mji_axisAngle2Quat` and `mju_mulQuat`; sim-core's kinematics use nalgebra. Porting only its normalizations moved the parity census both ways (3 more docs exact, 27 more farther), so it would be ported whole, measured with the census distance.
- **The hybrid under implicitspringdamper on a long chain** (ledger-L93). On a chain of 62 hinges the hybrid's `A` differs from pure finite differences by about 1e-4 with every spring and damper flag enabled; the excess grows as the finite-difference step shrinks, and the chain's masses barely move it. Not isolated. `implicitspringdamper_follows_the_spring_and_damper_flags` checks the hybrid on two hinges only.
- **The plugin state between finite-difference columns** (ledger-L89; by reading, not run). MuJoCo's `mjd_stepFD` restores `mjSTATE_FULLPHYSICS`, which holds the plugin state; sim-core's finite differences restore it only with sleep enabled (where each column copies the whole `Data`), so a plugin whose state a step advances would carry one column's state into the next.
