## Found while implementing, with no owner

Two differences from MuJoCo 3.5.0 that the review of Rigid-physics chunk 2b (P09–P14) found and no entry of 20-rigid-physics owns. Neither is isolated. Chunk 3 (P24–P53) assigns each an owner entry before its first commit.

- **Inverse finite differences under a constraint** (ledger-L66). The first model of `hybrid_takes_finite_differences_under_an_active_constraint` (`sim/L0/tests/integration/derivatives.rs`): a damped hinge at `qpos` 0.12, past its range ±0.1, one constraint row. Forward differences with eps 1e-6, measured at `main` `1bd103bc`: sim-core's `mjd_inverse_fd` gives DfDq 225.03, DfDv 9.0057, DfDa 0.090007; MuJoCo's `mjd_inverseFD` gives 4500.66, 180.115, 1.80015, each 20 times ours to eight significant digits. The forward states agree to 1e-13 (`qacc` −294.284, `qfrc_constraint` −28.9226 in both). The two take the constraint force differently: sim-core subtracts the force the forward solve left (`sim/L0/core/src/inverse.rs:52`), MuJoCo computes it from `qacc` (`mj_invConstraint`, `engine_inverse.c:223`); whether that accounts for the difference is not measured. Without a constraint they agree (`inverse_finite_differences_match_mujoco_3_5_0_without_constraints`). No entry owns inverse dynamics.
- **A box resting on a plane** (ledger-L68). Census doc `2529ce5bef3785f7`, the second model of the same test, verdict `e1:state@30`: under the first initial velocity, the state first differs at the step-30 checkpoint. Not isolated.

Found in Rigid-physics chunk 3a (the hybrid-versus-finite-differences sweep and its fixes), with no owner entry yet. Two more it found have owners, whose entries say what it measured: P30 (implicitfast and the mass matrix's sparsity) and P28 (the kinematics' quaternion arithmetic).

- **The plugin state between finite-difference columns** (ledger-L89; by reading, not run). MuJoCo's `mjd_stepFD` restores `mjSTATE_FULLPHYSICS`, which holds the plugin state; sim-core's finite differences restore it only with sleep enabled (where each perturbed step starts from a copy of the whole `Data`), so a plugin whose state a step advances would carry each step's state into the next.
- **Flex edge damping in the implicit integrators' `D`** (ledger-L94; by reading, not run). MuJoCo's `mjd_passive_vel` adds each flex's edge damping (`engine_derivative.c:1737-1758`); sim-core's (`derivatives/hybrid.rs`) has no flex term, while its passive pass applies the force (`forward/passive.rs:509`). So under implicitfast and implicit the step's `D`, and the hybrid's velocity columns, lack it.
- **The damper and eulerdamp flags outside the CPU step** (ledger-L95; by reading, not run). MuJoCo's Euler step applies the implicit joint damping only with both flags enabled (`engine_forward.c:956`), as sim-core's step does. sim-gpu's Euler damping runs whenever a dof has damping (`gpu/src/pipeline/orchestrator.rs:164`, `model_buffers.rs:193`), and sim-core's parameter Jacobians build `M + h·D` for the Euler step whatever the flags (`derivatives/param.rs`).
